# 🛒 E-Commerce Sales & Customer Analytics | Power BI

## 📌 Project Overview

This project analyzes e-commerce transaction data to understand **sales performance, customer behavior, product performance, payment preferences, and regional trends**.

An interactive **Power BI dashboard** was developed to transform raw transaction data into meaningful business insights and support data-driven decision-making.

## 🎯 Business Problem

The business needed a clear view of its e-commerce performance and wanted to understand:

* How sales and orders change over time
* Which product categories generate the highest sales
* Which payment methods customers prefer
* Which states contribute the most to sales
* New vs returning customer behavior
* Top-performing products
* Customer ratings across product categories

## 📊 Dataset

* **500 Orders**
* **220 Unique Customers**
* **10 Fields**
* **Transaction Period:** January–December 2025

### Key Columns

`Order_ID` | `Customer_ID` | `Age` | `Gender` | `State` | `Product_Category` | `Payment_Method` | `Order_Value` | `Customer_Rating` | `Order_Date`

## 💡 Key Business Insights

### 1. Beauty was the top-performing category

Beauty generated approximately **₹207.19K**, contributing around **20.2% of total sales**.

### 2. UPI was the leading payment method

UPI generated approximately **₹388K in sales**, making it the highest-contributing payment method.

### 3. Maharashtra recorded the highest sales

Maharashtra generated approximately **₹122.64K**, followed by Telangana and Delhi.

### 4. Sports had the highest customer rating

Sports recorded an average rating of approximately **4.07**.

### 5. Books had the lowest average rating

Books recorded an average rating of approximately **3.68**, highlighting an area for further investigation.

### 6. Top products contributed significantly to revenue

The top five products generated approximately **₹249.87K** in sales.

## 📌 Business Recommendations

* Focus promotional campaigns on high-performing categories such as Beauty.
* Continue optimizing the digital payment experience, particularly UPI.
* Target high-performing regions with localized campaigns.
* Investigate customer feedback for lower-rated categories.
* Promote top-performing products through recommendations and offers.
* Use loyalty strategies to encourage repeat purchases.
